# Best Free Startup Directories & Launch Platforms

> A hand-kept list of the places that are actually worth your time when you launch a startup, side project, or micro-SaaS — and want eyes on it without paying for the privilege.

There are *hundreds* of "submit your startup here" sites. Most are dead, spammy, or exist only to upsell you a $99 "we'll post to 200 directories" package. This list is the opposite: a curated set of platforms that have real audiences, real curation, or both — and where the core action (launching or getting listed) is free.

A quick reality check before you start submitting everywhere: **traffic from any single directory is usually modest.** The real value is compounding — backlinks for SEO, a handful of genuine early adopters, social proof badges, and the occasional post that catches fire. Treat it as distribution surface area, not a growth hack.

## Launch platforms

These run on a launch *date*. You post, the community votes, and you get a spike of attention, feedback, and often a badge to embed.

### Product Hunt
**[producthunt.com](https://www.producthunt.com)** · Free to launch

Still the default. A successful launch can put your product in front of tens of thousands of early adopters in a day. The catch: it's crowded, the algorithm rewards momentum, and your network and timing matter as much as the product. Worth doing once you've prepped — not something to wing on a Tuesday afternoon. Free to launch; paid promotion exists but isn't required.

### Hacker News — Show HN
**[news.ycombinator.com](https://news.ycombinator.com)** · Free

Not a directory — it's a single text post — but the highest-signal free launch venue there is for anything technical. A "Show HN" that resonates can drive a serious traffic spike *and* unusually thoughtful (read: harsh) feedback. No badges, no fluff, no gaming it. Best for dev tools, infra, and anything builders find genuinely clever.

### Tiny Startups
**[tinystartups.com](https://www.tinystartups.com)** · Free

A launch platform *and* a weekly newsletter read by ~20,000 bootstrapped founders, with the signature hook of describing products "in 10 words or less." You can submit your product, collect upvotes, and reach an audience that skews indie/bootstrapped rather than VC. Also maintains a directory of 100+ launch sites and a database of validated business ideas. Built by Jaisal Rathee as part of the Tiny Startup Studio.

### Uneed
**[uneed.best](https://www.uneed.best)** · Free (paid boosts)

A friendlier, lower-competition Product Hunt alternative aimed squarely at indie makers. Daily launch slots, a leaderboard, and a community that's less of a popularity contest. Free to launch with optional paid promotion if you want to skip the queue or feature higher.

### Peerlist
**[peerlist.io](https://peerlist.io)** · Free

A professional network for developers and makers with a built-in launchpad for projects. The audience is technical and engaged, and "build in public" culture is strong here. Good if your product appeals to other builders, and a nice complement to a Product Hunt launch rather than a replacement.

### BetaList
**[betalist.com](https://betalist.com)** · Free (paid skip-the-line)

The classic spot for *pre-launch* startups to collect early adopters and beta signups before you're fully live. The free queue can be long; there's a paid option to jump it. Best used early — while you still have a waitlist to fill — not after you've already launched everywhere else.

## Curated directories

Evergreen listings rather than dated launches. The payoff is long-tail discovery, SEO backlinks, and credibility badges. Get in once and you stay listed.

### Startups.fm
**[startups.fm](https://startups.fm)** · Free

A genuinely curated directory of 1,600+ hand-picked early-stage startups across AI, SaaS, DevTools, Fintech, and more — browsable by country, city, and category, and clearly tagged bootstrapped vs. VC-funded. Free to get listed, with an embeddable "Featured on Startups.fm" badge. One of the cleaner, more legit directories in this space.

### SaaS.fyi
**[saas.fyi](https://www.saas.fyi)** · Free

A human-curated directory of 600+ of the best SaaS tools across AI, sales, design, dev tools, and more. The selling point is the curation: every listing is read and written by a real person, with no pay-for-placement and no auto-generated filler. Free to browse; submit for review to get featured. More of a "best tools" reference than a launch platform, but a credible place to be listed.

### Best Startup Tools
**[beststartuptools.com](https://www.beststartuptools.com)** · Free

A live, upvote-ranked index of 1,100+ tools for founders, engineers, and operators — free and paid — hand-picked by humans. Beyond the leaderboard it has category filters, curated "stacks," and a quiz to help you assemble a setup. Free to browse, vote, and submit. Useful both as a place to get your tool listed and as a way to find your own stack.

### Startup Spotlight
**[startupspotlight.co](https://www.startupspotlight.co)** · Free

Founder-first discovery: spotlights on individual founders and the stories behind their products, aimed at the indie and bootstrapped ecosystem. A good fit if your traction story is the interesting part — the human angle rather than just a feature list. Part of the Tiny Startup Studio family of properties.

### Indie Hackers
**[indiehackers.com](https://www.indiehackers.com)** · Free

Less a directory, more a community with a product directory attached — and one of the best places on the internet for bootstrapped founders. You won't get a flood of traffic, but you'll get honest feedback, peers building similar things, and a culture of revenue transparency that's rare elsewhere. Worth a profile even if it never sends you a single user.

## Marketplaces & discovery indexes

### Buy Sell Startups — Startup Index
**[buysellstartups.com/startup-index](https://buysellstartups.com/startup-index)** · Free

The discovery layer of Buy Sell Startups, a 0%-commission marketplace for micro-startups, SaaS, and small internet businesses. The index lets you browse vetted listings and connect directly with founders — no brokers, no commission skimmed off the top. Most relevant if you're scouting what's being built (or what's for sale), but also a legitimate place to surface a profitable small product to the right next owner. Part of the Tiny Startup Studio family.

## How to actually use these

A few hard-won notes so you don't waste a weekend:

1. **Prep before you post.** A clear one-liner, a good thumbnail/logo, 2–3 screenshots, and a short "why I built this." Most launches die from sloppy assets, not bad products.
2. **Stagger your launches.** Don't blow every platform on the same day. Space them out so each gets its own spike and you have something to point to over weeks, not hours.
3. **Lead with the directories for SEO.** The evergreen listings (Startups.fm, SaaS.fyi, Best Startup Tools) keep working long after a launch-day spike fades — that's where the durable backlink value is.
4. **Reply to everything.** Comments and feedback in the first few hours drive ranking on most launch platforms *and* signal you're a real founder, not a drive-by submitter.
5. **Don't pay for "submit to 200 directories" services.** A handful of curated, real-audience listings beats 200 spam directories that Google already discounts.

## Contributing

Found a free directory or launch platform that genuinely deserves a spot? Open a PR. The bar:

- **Free** to launch or get listed (a paid boost tier is fine; pay-to-enter is not).
- **Real audience or real curation** — no autogenerated link farms.
- **Still alive** — check the last-updated date before suggesting it.

Add your entry under the right section, keep the description honest and to a couple of sentences, and note the cost clearly.
